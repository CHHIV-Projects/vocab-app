## 12. Small-vs-Large Architecture Analysis

Exhaustive Qwen is simplest and acceptable for most partitions when treated as versioned cached lexical preparation. Most partitions have one sense and require no pairwise judgment; 97% have three or fewer senses. Large partitions are rare but dominate worst-case workload, so latency mitigation should focus on async/background preparation and cache warming, not a universal screening layer.

## 13. Lazy-vs-Precompute Analysis

Lazy per-word consolidation is realistic for small/medium partitions and avoids whole-corpus inference. Cached repeat lookup requires zero Qwen calls. Large partitions may be too slow for synchronous UI and should be prepared asynchronously or warmed in the background if needed.

Full background precompute is operationally expensive: the scanned corpus projects **1,055,710** pair judgments before retries. At the measured p50 this is about 109 hours of local inference; at p95 about 982 hours. Full precompute should not be the first production posture.

## 16. Recommended Architecture Direction

Recommend **Outcome C: Candidate reduction remains unsafe**.

Production planning should prefer:

```text
canonical source senses
  -> deterministic POS/material partition
  -> exhaustive bounded Qwen single-pair semantic judgments
  -> Python-owned conservative complete-link clustering
  -> versioned/cacheable learner-meaning containers
```

Latency should be handled by cache identity, lazy computation, and optional background warming rather than an unsafe candidate-screening layer.
