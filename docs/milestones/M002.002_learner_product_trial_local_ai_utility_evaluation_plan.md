## Proposed corpus and selection method

The [60-word CSV](M002.002_learner_product_trial_local_ai_utility_evaluation_corpus.csv)
records normalized lemma, POS counts, total, stratum, rationale and subset membership.
It is a designed stratified trial, **not a probability sample of learner queries**.
Do not infer population failure prevalence from its aggregate.

Selection uses no new model output or success/failure score. Two explicit
human-readable seed pools were chosen before querying lexical counts. Seed-pool
choice remains Coder-proposed, not independently established learner frequency;
PO review may replace entries before freezing the corpus.

**Normal pool, in fixed order:**

```text
apple bread chair cloud door garden hill island milk moon rain river road school
seed stone tree window brave calm gentle honest narrow patient quiet simple useful
vivid warm worry admire borrow improve notice prefer repair remember explain accept
```

Choose the first 30 entries whose complete current lexical source count is 1..20.
This covers ordinary concrete/abstract nouns, actions and qualities, including
one-sense and multi-POS entries. Counts are modest relative to the complexity
stratum, not guaranteed small. Do not delete source senses to fit the threshold.

**Complexity pool:**

```text
bank bark bat beam break charge clear cold draft draw fair field fine hold issue
light match plant point right ring rock run scale seal set spring staff strike table
turn watch wave well
```

Sort this 34-word pool by `(total_source_senses, normalized_lemma)`. Choose indices
`floor(i * (34 - 1) / (20 - 1))` for `i=0..19`, including endpoints. The spread
includes moderate ambiguity, related/distinct senses and extreme multi-sense stress.
Do not replace expensive or failed cases after observing output.

**Challenge pool:** lantern, jelly, cobble, phone, vertigo, flummox, quartz,
archipelago, lead, sanction. The first eight are prompt-named cases, not all known
semantic failures. Lantern is the accepted scaffold-shift regression. Lead adds
heteronym/multi-POS stress; sanction adds contrasting noun/verb meanings.

| Normal | 30 | 335 | 1..20 |
| Complexity | 20 | 1027 | 14..156 |
| Challenge | 10 | 129 | 2..67 |
| Total | 60 | 1491 | 1..156 |

Counts come from immutable SQLite reads of the current active projection:
`/home/chuck/.local/share/vocab-lexical/versions/wiktionary-20260901/lexical.sqlite`,
dataset `wiktionary-20260901`, importer `m004.3-v1`.
They aggregate **all** entries at the normalized lemma by source POS, matching
current lookup behavior. Proper names, symbols, specialist/obsolete senses and

This can increase task difficulty even in normal learner words; report it rather
than reclassifying after generation. Supplemental WordNet counts are not included.

Metadata query: `entries LEFT JOIN senses ON entry_key`, exact normalized-lemma
