## 4. Final Architectural Question

The architectural question tested in M004.6.13.3 was whether stripping product-policy action decisions (`MERGE` vs `KEEP-SEPARATE`) from the LLM prompt and asking the model *only* to classify the semantic relationship between dictionary senses would eliminate structural coupling errors (`merge / specialized_subsense`) and yield a safe, reliable consolidation signal.

In this architecture:
- **AI owns:** Semantic relationship classification.
- **Code owns:** Policy, topology, completeness, identity, persistence, and action mapping.
