## 4. Word-Level Integration Architecture

[vocab_consolidation_workflow.py](/home/chuck/projects/vocab-app/vocab_consolidation_workflow.py)
composes the accepted M004.6.9 (`vocab_consolidation.build_partitions`), M004.6.10
(`ConsolidationPersistence`), and M004.6.11 (`run_partition_consolidation` orchestrator)
primitives into one word-level entry point:

- `plan_word_consolidation(evidence)` — deterministic, zero-cost partition discovery.
- `total_expected_pair_count(partitions)` — deterministic pre-inference work-size calculation.
- `consolidate_word_evidence(persistence, evidence, provider, *, model_identity,
  semantic_options, max_total_pairs=None)` — the bounded, cache-aware, resumable word-level
  driver. Returns a `WordConsolidationResult` aggregating a `PartitionConsolidationStatus` per
  partition.

No new provider or orchestration logic was introduced; the module is purely a composition
layer, unchanged since before the suspension.

## 5. Input Evidence Boundary

Canonical evidence enters via the already-accepted `project_synthesis_evidence()` adapter
(same evidence shape consumed by the existing learner-facing synthesis path), so
`consolidate_word_evidence()` never re-derives or bypasses the canonical lexical evidence
projection.

## 6. Partition Planning

`plan_word_consolidation()` calls the unmodified `build_partitions()` (M004.6.9) to discover
all deterministic `(pos, material_partition)` partitions for a word, each carrying its own
`partition_id`, member senses, and `expected_pair_count` (0 for one-sense partitions,
`C(n,2)` otherwise). Verified against real data throughout this milestone (`gecko`: 3
partitions, `lynx`/`heron`/`otter`: 2 partitions each with exactly 1 non-trivial pair).

## 7. Result Contract

`WordConsolidationResult`: `normalized_lemma`, `word_status` (`"complete"` / `"incomplete"` /
`"blocked"`), `partitions: tuple[PartitionConsolidationStatus, ...]`,
`total_provider_calls_made`, `total_reused_pair_count`, `total_expected_pair_count`.
`PartitionConsolidationStatus`: `partition_id`, `pos`, `material_partition`, `status`
(`"topology_validated"` / `"already_complete"` / `"incomplete"` / `"blocked_terminal_failure"`),
`provider_calls_made`, `topology_identity`, `containers`. Unchanged since before suspension.

## 8. Ordering

Partitions are processed and reported in `build_partitions()`'s own deterministic
`(pos, material_partition, partition_id)` ordering — not the learner-facing
`POS_PRESENTATION_ORDER` — confirmed stable and reproducible by
`test_stable_result_ordering_is_reproducible` and observed directly in every live JSON report
in this milestone (`gecko`: name, noun, verb; `lynx`/`heron`: name, noun).

## 9. Cache Reuse

`consolidate_word_evidence()` calls `create_or_get_job()` per partition; if a compatible job is
already `topology_validated`, the partition is reported `already_complete` with
`provider_calls_made=0` and the persisted `topology_identity`/`containers` are returned without
touching the provider. Directly proven live: the second `lynx` invocation reported both
partitions `already_complete`, `total_provider_calls_made=0`.

## 10. Resume Behavior

Partial jobs (some pairs `succeeded_validated`, others `pending`/`failed_retryable`) resume via
the M004.6.11 orchestrator's existing lease/claim logic — already-succeeded pairs are never
re-submitted to the provider. Directly proven live: the second and third `gecko`-noun
invocations only ever re-attempted the two pairs that were not yet `succeeded_validated`
(`id 17`, `id 19`); the already-`succeeded_validated` pair (`id 18`) was never re-claimed or
re-billed against the Qwen call budget.

## 13. Bounded Execution

`max_total_pairs` is a hard, required (for non-`--plan-only` runs) ceiling on live provider
calls across all partitions in one invocation — enforced by
`tools/dev/consolidate_word.py`'s `parser.error(...)` guard and by
`consolidate_word_evidence()`'s internal budget decrement. Every live invocation in this
milestone passed an explicit, small `--max-total-pairs` value; no unbounded run was ever made.

## 14. Incomplete / Blocked States

- A partition with unresolved pending/retryable pairs after budget exhaustion reports
  `"incomplete"` (e.g. `gecko`/noun after its first live invocation, `heron`/name after its
  single-call invocation).
- A partition whose only remaining pairs reach `failed_terminal` (retry-exhausted) reports
  `"blocked_terminal_failure"` and the whole word reports `word_status="blocked"` — proven live:
  `gecko`'s noun partition reached `failed_terminal` on both of its remaining pairs after 3
  attempts each, and the overall `gecko` word result correctly reported `"blocked"` even though
  its other two partitions were fully `topology_validated`. This matches the coverage invariant
  from M004.6.9/M004.6.10 (terminal judgments block topology finalization) exactly as designed
  — not a defect.
